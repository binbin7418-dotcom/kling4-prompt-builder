FAQ

What is the official Kling 4.0 prompt structure?

Four parts: asset roles (what each reference controls), global settings (scene, texture, style, camera tone), a sequence of timed events, and an audio line. This builder follows it exactly.

Keyframes or Omni Reference — which do I pick?

Keyframes when specific moments must look exactly like your images (product reveals, makeovers, entrances). Omni Reference when identity and elements must stay consistent across a freer scene. Never both in one generation.

How long can a Kling 4.0 clip be?

Up to 30 seconds, which is why timed beats matter: at 30 seconds a single-action prompt drags. Plan a beat every 4–8 seconds.

Why does my prompt get subtitles or music I didn't ask for?

The model adds both by default. Kling's own guide recommends the two negatives "no subtitles" and "no background music" — the builder adds them for you.

How long can a Kling 4.0 video be?

Up to 30 seconds in a single generation — long enough for multi-beat scenes. The builder checks your beat timings against your total duration so no beat gets silently truncated.

How many keyframes can Kling 4.0 use?

Up to 10 keyframes per generation. Omni Reference mode is the alternative: it accepts up to 15 reference assets instead. The two modes cannot be combined in one generation.

What is Omni Reference in Kling 4.0?

Omni Reference locks identity and style by referencing up to 15 assets — characters, products or scenes — so the same subject stays consistent across shots and clips.

How do I keep a character consistent across Kling 4.0 shots?

Reuse the same asset block word for word in every shot prompt, and use Omni Reference with the same reference set. Describe the character once, identically, in each prompt; do not paraphrase the description between shots.

What audio can Kling 4.0 generate?

Native ambience, sound effects and lip-synced dialogue cues can be written into the beats of your prompt. If you plan to add music in editing, include "no background music" as a negative prompt.

Why does the builder warn me about a beat's length?

Because Kling renders what the timing allows: a beat whose action and dialogue need about 6 seconds will be cut off in a 4-second shot. The warning means the beat's word count implies a longer shot than the one you gave it.
