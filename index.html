FAQ

What is the official Kling 4.0 prompt structure?

Four parts: asset roles (what each reference controls), global settings (scene, texture, style, camera tone), a sequence of timed events, and an audio line. This builder follows it exactly.

Keyframes or Omni Reference — which do I pick?

Keyframes when specific moments must look exactly like your images (product reveals, makeovers, entrances). Omni Reference when identity and elements must stay consistent across a freer scene. Never both in one generation.

How long can a Kling 4.0 clip be?

Up to 30 seconds, which is why timed beats matter: at 30 seconds a single-action prompt drags. Plan a beat every 4–8 seconds.

Why does my prompt get subtitles or music I didn't ask for?

The model adds both by default. Kling's own guide recommends the two negatives "no subtitles" and "no background music" — the builder adds them for you.
